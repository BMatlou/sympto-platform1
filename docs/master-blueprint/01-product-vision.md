# 1. Executive Summary

Sympto is an AI-powered Digital Health Intelligence Platform designed to provide individuals, healthcare practitioners and healthcare organisations with a secure, connected and intelligent healthcare ecosystem.

Rather than functioning only as a symptom tracker, Sympto serves as a lifelong digital health companion by combining personal health records, wearable technology, artificial intelligence, secure collaboration and remote patient monitoring.

The platform enables patients to understand their health through trends and personalised insights while empowering healthcare professionals with accurate, permission-based clinical information that supports better decision-making.

Sympto is designed to be interoperable with existing healthcare systems, wearable devices, laboratories, pharmacies and future healthcare technologies while maintaining the highest standards of privacy, security and user control.

# 4. Product Philosophy

Sympto is built around five principles:

## 4.1 Patient Ownership

Patients own their health information.

Patients decide:

* Who may access their records.
* Which records may be shared.
* How long access remains active.
* When access can be revoked.

No healthcare provider, organisation or third party owns a patient's health information.

---

## 4.2 AI Assists — It Does Not Diagnose

Artificial Intelligence within Sympto exists to support better healthcare decisions.

The AI will:

* Analyse trends.
* Identify possible risks.
* Generate personalised recommendations.
* Explain every recommendation.
* Encourage early medical attention where appropriate.

The AI will never claim to diagnose disease or replace qualified healthcare professionals.

---

## 4.3 Prevention Before Treatment

The platform prioritises prevention through:

* Trend analysis
* Lifestyle coaching
* Medication adherence
* Remote monitoring
* Early warning alerts
* Preventive health recommendations

The goal is to identify meaningful changes early and encourage timely engagement with healthcare providers.

---

## 4.4 Privacy by Design

Privacy is built into every layer of the platform.

Sympto is designed to align with:

* POPIA (South Africa)
* GDPR (European Union)

The platform will also follow recognised healthcare security practices and support compliance requirements in additional jurisdictions as the platform expands.

---

## 4.5 Explainable Intelligence

Every AI recommendation must answer:

* Why was this recommendation generated?
* Which information contributed?
* Which trends were detected?
* What action is recommended?
* What level of confidence is available?
* What information should be discussed with a healthcare professional?

Users should always understand how recommendations are produced.

# 8. Personal Health Passport

Every patient has a secure Digital Health Passport.

The passport may include:

* Blood group
* Allergies
* Current medications
* Chronic conditions
* Emergency contacts
* Insurance information
* Medical aid information
* Primary healthcare practitioner
* Recent prescriptions
* Vaccination history
* Medical alerts
* Emergency medical notes

The patient controls what is shared.

The passport can be shared using:

* Secure QR Code
* Secure temporary link
* Practitioner invitation

Access automatically expires unless renewed.

---

# 9. Family Health Hub

Sympto supports linked family accounts.

Examples include:

* Parents managing children.
* Adults assisting elderly parents.
* Caregivers supporting dependants.

Every relationship is governed by explicit permissions.

Patients can:

* Grant access.
* Limit access.
* Revoke access.

All access is recorded in the audit log.

---

# 10. Ethical Research Participation

Users may voluntarily contribute anonymised health information to approved research programmes.

Participation is:

* Optional.
* Based on informed consent.
* Revocable at any time.

Personally identifying information is protected before any authorised research use.

# 14. Long-Term Vision

Sympto will become a globally connected Digital Health Intelligence Platform supporting patients, practitioners, healthcare organisations and connected medical devices.

The platform will remain modular, secure and interoperable so that new AI capabilities, wearable technologies, healthcare providers, pharmacies, laboratories and future Sympto devices can be added without redesigning the core platform.

The long-term objective is to improve health outcomes through proactive, connected, explainable and patient-centred healthcare while preserving privacy, trust and user ownership of health information.
